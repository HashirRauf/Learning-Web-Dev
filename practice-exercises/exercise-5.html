107th Infantry Division (German Empire)

From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The 107th Infantry Division (107. Infanterie-Division) was a unit of the Imperial German Army in World War I.
The division was formed on June 1, 1915, and organized over the next few weeks.It was part of a wave of new infantry divisions formed in 1915.
The division was disbanded in 1919 during the demobilization of the German Army after World War I.
The division was formed primarily from the excess infantry regiments of existing divisions which were being triangularized.
The division's 213th Infantry Brigade was formerly the 98th Reserve Infantry Brigade of the 49th Reserve Division, and came to the division with the 227th Reserve Infantry Regiment.

Order of battle on formation

The 107th Infantry Division was formed as a triangular division. The order of battle of the division on June 3, 1915, was as follows:

  • 213. Infanterie-Brigade

    • Reserve-Infanterie-Regiment Nr. 227
    • Reserve-Infanterie-Regiment Nr. 232
  • 1.Eskadron/Kürassier-Regiment Nr. 6
  • 2.Eskadron/Kürassier-Regiment Nr. 6
  • Feldartillerie-Regiment Nr. 213
  • Fußartillerie-Batterie Nr. 107
  • Pionier-Kompanie Nr. 213
